What is LikHabi?

LikHabi is a Filipino heritage clothing brand that turns traditional patterns and textile stories into everyday T-shirts. The name joins likha, to create, and habi, to weave.

Who we serve

People who want clothes with meaning: everyday wearers who take pride in where they come from, and the indigenous and artisan communities whose designs we work with.

Our advocacy

A portion of every purchase goes to our advocacy partner for community building and culture preservation, to help keep Filipino weaving traditions alive.

About Us

LikHabi Collectives

A Filipino heritage clothing brand that turns traditional patterns and textile stories into everyday T-shirts. Every piece is made to be worn with pride, to celebrate where we come from and to help keep Filipino weaving traditions alive.

Our designs belong to the indigenous and artisan communities who are our partners. Because pieces are made by hand through weaving, small variations in design, color and size are natural features of each product.
